About the Artist

the story behind the portraits

Hi, I'm Claire! I'm a charcoal portrait artist based in Wisconsin. When I'm not at my day job, you'll find me at my drawing table, capturing the spirit of beloved pets one portrait at a time.

Every animal has a story written in the curve of their ears, the tilt of their head, and the light in their eyes. My goal is to capture those fleeting expressions that make your pet uniquely them—the ones that make you smile every time you see them.

Why Charcoal?

There's something magical about charcoal as a medium. It's raw, expressive, and beautifully imperfect—just like the animals I draw.

I work with black, white, and colored charcoal, which allows me to build up rich, velvety darks, lift out delicate highlights, and add subtle warmth and tonal depth. The texture of fur, the gleam in an eye, the softness of a muzzle—charcoal captures it all with a timeless, classic feel.

My Approach

Every portrait I create begins with getting to know your pet. I want to hear the stories—the quirky habits, the favorite napping spots, the way they greet you at the door. These details don't just help me choose the right reference photo; they help me understand the soul I'm trying to capture.

I believe a great pet portrait should do more than look like your pet. It should feel like them. When you look at it, you should hear their bark, feel their fur, remember their warmth.

That's what I strive for with every piece.
